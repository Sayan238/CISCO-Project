CASE ID: NET-001

TITLE:
Wrong VLAN Assignment

SYMPTOM:
PC1 cannot reach its expected default gateway.

TOPOLOGY:
PC1 -> Switch0 -> Router0

PC1 NETWORK:
IP Address: 192.168.10.10
Subnet Mask: 255.255.255.0
Default Gateway: 192.168.10.1

EXPECTED VLAN:
VLAN 10 - STUDENT

ACTUAL SWITCH PORT:
FastEthernet0/1

ACTUAL VLAN:
VLAN 20 - STAFF

EVIDENCE COMMAND:
show vlan brief

EVIDENCE:
VLAN 10 STUDENT is active.
VLAN 20 STAFF is active.
FastEthernet0/1 is assigned to VLAN 20.
FastEthernet0/2 is assigned to VLAN 20.
FastEthernet0/3 is assigned to VLAN 30.

TRUNK EVIDENCE:
Command: show interfaces trunk

FastEthernet0/4 is trunking.
Encapsulation: 802.1Q
Native VLAN: 1
VLANs allowed on trunk: 1-1005
VLANs allowed and active: 1,10,20,30

EXPECTED FAULT:
FastEthernet0/1 is assigned to VLAN 20, but PC1 should be in VLAN 10.

OSI LAYER:
Layer 2

CONCEPT:
VLAN

SEVERITY:
High

EXPECTED NEXT COMMAND:
show vlan brief

EXPECTED FIX:
Configure FastEthernet0/1 as an access port in VLAN 10.

FIX COMMAND:
interface fastEthernet 0/1
switchport mode access
switchport access vlan 10
