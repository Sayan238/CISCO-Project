CASE ID: NET-004

TITLE:
VLAN 30 Not Allowed on Trunk

SYMPTOM:
The device connected to Switch0 Fa0/3 cannot communicate with the VLAN 30 gateway.

TOPOLOGY:
Device/Server1 -> Switch0 Fa0/3 -> Switch0 Fa0/4 -> Router0

EXPECTED VLAN:
VLAN 30 - SERVER

EXPECTED PORT:
FastEthernet0/3

TRUNK PORT:
FastEthernet0/4

EXPECTED CONFIGURATION:
Fa0/4 should be a trunk and should allow VLAN 30.

FAULT INTRODUCED:
VLAN 30 was removed from the allowed VLAN list on Switch0 Fa0/4.

FAULT COMMAND:
interface fastEthernet 0/4
switchport trunk allowed vlan 1,10,20

EVIDENCE COMMAND:
show interfaces trunk

EVIDENCE:

Fa0/4 is still operating as a trunk.

Fa0/4:
Mode: on
Encapsulation: 802.1q
Status: trunking
Native VLAN: 1

VLANs allowed on trunk:
1,10,20

VLAN 30 is NOT allowed on the trunk.

VLAN 30 still exists on the switch:

VLAN 30 SERVER active Fa0/3

Therefore, the problem is not that VLAN 30 is missing.
The problem is that VLAN 30 is not allowed on the trunk.

VLAN TABLE EVIDENCE:
Command:
show vlan brief

VLAN 10 STUDENT - active - Fa0/1
VLAN 20 STAFF - active - Fa0/2
VLAN 30 SERVER - active - Fa0/3

TRUNK EVIDENCE:
Command:
show interfaces trunk

Fa0/4:
Vlans allowed on trunk:
1,10,20

VLAN 30 is missing from the allowed VLAN list.

EXPECTED FAULT:
VLAN 30 is not allowed on the Fa0/4 trunk.

OSI LAYER:
Layer 2

CONCEPT:
VLAN Trunking / 802.1Q

SEVERITY:
High

EXPECTED NEXT COMMAND:
show interfaces trunk

EXPECTED FIX:
Allow VLAN 30 on Switch0 Fa0/4 trunk.

FIX COMMAND:
configure terminal
interface fastEthernet 0/4
switchport trunk allowed vlan 1,10,20,30
end

EXPECTED RESULT AFTER FIX:
VLAN 30 should appear in the allowed VLAN list.
VLAN 30 should appear in the active VLAN list on the trunk.
VLAN 30 should appear in the spanning-tree forwarding VLAN list.
Fa0/4 should remain in trunking state.
